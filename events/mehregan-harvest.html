Harvest & Gratitude

📅 Saturday, October 10, 2026 🕔 2:00 – 4:00 PM 📍 Lynn Valley Program Room, Lynn Valley Library
  • For kids 5 to 10
  • Free event
  • Registration required — spots are limited
  • All materials provided

A hands-on workshop for children ages 5–10 celebrating Thanksgiving and the harvest festival of northern Iran through stories, gratitude crafts, a short collaborative drama and a traditional dance.

About the Workshop

  • A shared harvest celebration: Explore the connection between Thanksgiving and the Harvest Festival (Jashn-e Bardasht) traditions of northern Iran.
  • Storytelling journey: Children will go on a storytelling journey through the traditions, ceremonies, and stories connected to both celebrations.
  • Gratitude craft: Inspired by the stories, each child will create an element of gratitude, such as a rice doll or a “Thanks” leaf.
  • Cardboard scenery & props: Children will use a handmade cardboard setting—including trees, houses, kitchen and cooking elements, and a feast/harvest scene—to bring their story to life during the collaborative drama.
  • Collaborative drama: Working together, children will create and perform a short drama based on the story they heard, incorporating the cardboard scenery and the crafts they have made.
  • Harvest celebration: The workshop will end with a small snack and a short traditional dance from northern Iran, reflecting the dancing that commonly follows harvest celebrations.

👧🧒 For Children Ages 5–10

  • This is a children-only workshop.
  • Parents and guardians will not participate in the workshop activities.
  • Parents/guardians are asked to remain nearby and be available in case their child needs assistance.

📋 Important for Parents & Guardians

  • A waiver and release form will be emailed to the child's guardian the night before the workshop.
  • A signed waiver is required for every participating child before they can take part.
  • Please make sure you are available to respond promptly if your child needs you during the workshop.
