Harvest & Gratitude

📅 Saturday, October 10, 2026 🕔 2:00 – 4:00 PM 📍 Lynn Valley Program Room, Lynn Valley Library
  • For kids 5 to 10
  • Free event
  • Registration required — spots are limited
  • All materials provided

A hands-on workshop for children ages 5–10 celebrating Thanksgiving and the harvest festival of northern Iran through stories, gratitude crafts, a short collaborative drama and a traditional dance.

About the Workshop

Harvest & Gratitude is a shared harvest celebration that brings together Thanksgiving traditions with the Harvest Festival (Jashn-e-Barshat) traditions of northern Iran. During this hands-on workshop, children will take a journey through stories and traditions from both celebrations. Along the way, they will create their own gratitude crafts—such as a rice doll or a “thanks” leaf—inspired by what they learn. The children will then work together to create and perform a short collaborative drama based on the story they heard, using a cardboard stage and decorations provided for the workshop and enhanced with their new handmade crafts. The celebration will conclude with a small snack and a short traditional dance from northern Iran, reflecting the dancing that commonly follows harvest celebrations.

This is a children-only workshop for ages 5–10. Parents and guardians are not part of the workshop activities, but we ask that they remain nearby and be available to assist promptly if their child needs them. A waiver and release form will be sent to the guardian by email the night before the workshop. A signed form is required for every participating child before they can take part.
